CareTrack

An Android app that keeps track of your Product Care plans and manufacturer warranties, and reminds you before the cover ends.

Privacy policy Contact

What it does

  • Reads your tax invoiceScan a paper invoice, open the PDF, or share it from your email. CareTrack reads it on your phone and fills in each product, its warranty and its Product Care.
  • Reminds you in timeNotifications before a warranty or Product Care plan ends, plus a "test it before it expires" checklist of common faults for that kind of product.
  • Keeps your evidenceDate-stamped fault photos and notes, your invoice and serial numbers, all in one place for a claim.
  • Finds invoice emailsIf you link your Gmail (or Outlook) account, CareTrack looks for Harvey Norman eReceipts and offers to add them. Read-only.
  • Syncs between your devicesSign in with your Google account and your data is kept in a private CareTrack folder in your own Google Drive.
  • MoreNearest store and opening hours, product recall alerts, an insurance inventory and a home-screen widget.
Your data stays yours. CareTrack has no servers of its own. Everything is kept on your phone and, if you choose, in your own Google account. Read the privacy policy.

About

CareTrack is a small, independent app. It is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with Harvey Norman, Assurant or any manufacturer. Always rely on your invoice and Product Care booklet for the exact terms of your cover.

Contact

Questions, problems or requests to remove data: open an issue on GitHub, or use the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen when you link your account.
