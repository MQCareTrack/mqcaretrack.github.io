Privacy policy

CareTrack keeps your information on your phone and in your own accounts. It has no servers of its own, shows no ads and has no analytics or tracking.

CareTrack home Contact

Effective 11 October 2026. This policy covers the CareTrack Android app (package com.maqls.caretrack).

What CareTrack stores

The products you add: names, product codes, serial and IMEI numbers, purchase and delivery dates, prices, warranty and Product Care details, invoice numbers, the store you bought from and the name printed on the invoice. Also your invoice files, product and fault photos, videos and notes, and your settings.

All of this is stored in CareTrack's private storage on your phone. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Google account data

Linking a Google account is optional. CareTrack only asks for the access a feature needs, and only when you turn that feature on.

PermissionUsed forWhat CareTrack does with it
Google Drive: its own app folder only
drive.appdata		 Sync between your devices Saves a copy of your CareTrack data and files in a hidden CareTrack folder in your own Google Drive, and reads it back on your other devices. CareTrack can't see any other files in your Drive.
Gmail: read only
gmail.readonly		 Finding Harvey Norman invoice emails Searches only for emails from eReceipt@au.harveynorman.com, downloads those emails and their PDF invoices to your phone, and reads the invoice details on your phone so you can add the products. CareTrack never reads other emails for any other purpose, and can't send, change or delete email.

Google data is accessed directly from your phone to Google. It never passes through a server run by the developer, and it is never sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

CareTrack's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Microsoft (Outlook) account data

If you link an Outlook or Hotmail account, CareTrack asks Microsoft for read-only mail access (Mail.Read) and your basic profile (to show which address is linked). It is used in the same limited way as Gmail: only emails from eReceipt@au.harveynorman.com are downloaded, directly to your phone. The sign-in token is kept in CareTrack's private storage on your phone.

Other permissions

  • Camera and photos: to scan invoices and record fault evidence. Pictures stay in CareTrack.
  • Location (only if you allow it): to sort Harvey Norman stores by distance. Your location is used on your phone and not sent anywhere.
  • Calendar (only if you turn it on): to add cover end dates to the calendar you choose.
  • Notifications: for reminders, recall alerts and new-invoice alerts.

Information CareTrack downloads

CareTrack downloads public information without sending any of your details: store locations and opening hours from Harvey Norman's public store pages, and product recall notices from Product Safety Australia. Matching recalls against your products happens on your phone.

Sharing

CareTrack does not share your information with anyone. Data only leaves your phone when you choose to: syncing to your own Google Drive, saving or sharing a backup, PDF or spreadsheet, or opening a link.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete a product in CareTrack to remove it and its files (on all your synced devices).
  • Uninstalling CareTrack removes everything stored on that phone.
  • To delete the synced copy, open Google Drive on the web → Settings → Manage apps → CareTrack → Delete hidden app data.
  • To remove CareTrack's access to your Google account, visit myaccount.google.com/connections. For Microsoft, visit account.live.com/consent/Manage.

Children

CareTrack is intended for adults managing their own purchases. It isn't directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open an issue on GitHub, or use the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen when you link your account.
